Almubadir Uploader

A private publishing tool for the AlMubadir YouTube channel

What it is

Almubadir Uploader is a small, private tool used by Mounir, the creator and owner of the YouTube channel «المبادر | AlMubadir» (@AlMubadir9), to manage his own channel. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, it has no user accounts, and it is not offered to other people.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's videos to his own YouTube channel, as private.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedules for those videos.
  • Changes the privacy status of the owner's videos and adds them to the owner's playlists, when he asks for it.
  • Reads the owner's channel and video information to check that each action is applied to the correct channel.
  • Optionally, when the owner enables it: reads his own YouTube Analytics reports to prepare a media kit, and saves that media kit as files in his own Google Drive.
The tool only works on the YouTube channel and Google account of the person who signs in, which is the channel owner. It does not access anyone else's account.

Privacy Policy

Almubadir Uploader · Effective date: 22 October 2026

1. Who we are

Almubadir Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used by Mounir, the owner of the YouTube channel «المبادر | AlMubadir» (@AlMubadir9), to manage his own channel. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users. Contact: almubadir.business@gmail.com.

2. Google user data the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the tool can access data only for that signed-in account and its YouTube channel:

Permission (scope)What the tool uses it for
youtube.uploadUploading the owner's video files to his channel.
youtube.readonlyReading the owner's channel and video details (for example the channel ID, video status and processing state), to confirm that each action is applied to the correct channel and video.
youtube.force-sslManaging the owner's own videos: setting thumbnails, updating titles, descriptions and tags, changing privacy status and publish schedules, and adding videos to his playlists. This permission also allows deleting videos. The tool deletes a video only when the owner explicitly asks for that specific video.
yt-analytics.readonly (optional)Only if the owner enables the media kit feature: reading his own channel's YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time, subscribers and audience age, gender and country, to build a media kit about his channel.
drive.file (optional)Only if the owner enables the media kit feature: creating and updating the media kit PDF files in his own Google Drive. This permission is limited to files the tool creates.

The tool does not request access to Gmail, contacts, location, payment information, or any other Google service.

3. How the data is used

  • Only to perform the actions the owner asks for on his own channel and in his own Drive, as listed above.
  • The tool does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to build profiles of anyone.
  • The tool does not transfer Google user data to any third party. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Google's own services (YouTube, YouTube Analytics and Google Drive).
  • Media kit reports contain channel-level statistics only, aggregated by YouTube. They are saved privately in the owner's Drive and are shared only if the owner decides to share them himself.

4. Storage and security

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by his user account. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the tool, because the tool has no server.
  • Video files, metadata files and generated reports stay on the owner's computer or in his own Google account.
  • The tool does not keep copies of Google user data anywhere else.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Sign-in tokens remain on the owner's computer until he revokes access or deletes the token files.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the tool can no longer access the account.
  • Local files created by the tool can be deleted by the owner at any time.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The tool is used only by the adult channel owner. It is not directed to children.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: almubadir.business@gmail.com.
